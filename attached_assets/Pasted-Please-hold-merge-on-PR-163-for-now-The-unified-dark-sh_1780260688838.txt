Please hold merge on PR #163 for now. The unified dark shell is a good direction, but the current result is not coherent enough yet.

High-level read:

The Management page is the strongest design direction. It feels like the actual TIBER product cockpit: dark, focused, ember-accented, card-based, and action-oriented.

The rest of the app currently feels like old pages placed inside a dark wrapper. I want a universal TIBER experience, not just a dark topbar over mixed-era components.

What is working:

* The fixed dark topbar is a good direction.
* TIBER | [section] is a good universal identity pattern.
* The darker theme is the right product direction.
* Management feels closest to the target experience.
* The app should not flip between cream/light pages and dark Command Deck pages.

What is not coherent yet:

1. Mixed component eras
    Some pages still contain light cards, white pills, white dropdowns, old table styles, and old spacing. This breaks the unified dark product feel.

Examples:

* Rankings has a white Alpha ↓ dropdown on a dark page.
* Rookies has a white generated-date card and white Phase pill.
* Some tables/cards look like legacy light components dropped onto a dark background.

2. Management is the visual source of truth, but other pages do not follow it yet
    Use the Management dashboard visual language as the design anchor:

* dark cards
* subtle borders
* ember accents
* muted secondary text
* no pure white controls unless deliberately used as a rare highlight
* clear card hierarchy
* sober command-system feel

3. Mobile layout needs tightening
    The screenshots are mobile. The app should feel mobile-legible:

* no clipped tables unless intentionally horizontally scrollable with a visible container
* no oversized pills/cards breaking layout
* sidebar drawer should feel controlled and polished
* route content should not sit awkwardly under/behind the drawer or topbar
* spacing should be consistent across pages

4. Observatory copy/design needs product polish
    The Observatory page looks strong visually, but the copy still feels very operator/internal. Keep the read-only / inspection idea, but make it feel like a product surface, not a doctrine page.
5. Dark shell should not be a one-PR global repaint unless component states are fixed
    The direction is good, but please finish the obvious theme mismatches before merge.
