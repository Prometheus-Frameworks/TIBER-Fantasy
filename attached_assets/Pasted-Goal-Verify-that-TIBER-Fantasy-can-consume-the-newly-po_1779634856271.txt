Goal

Verify that TIBER-Fantasy can consume the newly populated TIBER-Data player_ownership_v0 artifact and return useful roster-truth coverage for the 26-player dynasty roster smoke test.

This is the first end-to-end usability check for the original question: can TIBER begin helping with a real fantasy roster?

Background

TIBER-Data now has a populated ownership artifact for the 26-player dynasty roster smoke test. TIBER-Fantasy has a read-only player ownership consumer and GET /api/data-lab/player-ownership endpoint.

The next step is to prove the downstream surface can recognize the full roster and preserve confidence/warning semantics:

source_verified draft rows should remain source-verified.
stale nflreadpy roster-snapshot rows should surface as provisional/stale-current-roster warnings.
unmatched/ambiguous cases should remain explicit.
TIBER-Fantasy should not invent or override ownership truth.
